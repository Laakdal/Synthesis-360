opencascade-rs bindings
-----------------------
CAD360 includes a modified copy of the "opencascade-sys" and "opencascade" crates, version
0.3.0, from the opencascade-rs project by Brian Schwind and contributors
(https://github.com/bschwind/opencascade-rs), licensed under LGPL-2.1. CAD360's changes
